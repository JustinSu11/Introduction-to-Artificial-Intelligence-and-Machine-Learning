# Lab 1.4: Staying Current with SCP
*AAI-501 · Read time: 3 min*

**One line:** AI moves fast. Beat obsolescence with a
simple habit: **Search -> Collect -> Process.**

## The assignment (this week and every week)
- **Week 1:** pick **5 sources** to monitor and write
  your **collect workflow** (how you gather and review
  them).
- **Every week:** review **one AI article** published in
  the last week.
- SCP is a suggestion. You can use your own system, but
  make it a sustainable habit and explain it.

## Why bother
The field is huge and fast. Staying current keeps your
career relevant and the work fun.
Without a system you waste time, miss insights, and
lose motivation.

## SCP at a glance
| Step | Goal |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Find high-quality sources |
| **Collect** | Pull them into one place |
| **Process** | Read at the right depth |

**Hook:** Find it -> Funnel it -> Filter it.

## 1. Search: 4 buckets of sources
(The lecturer's picks. Yours may differ.)
1. **Tech company blogs:** Meta AI blog, Netflix Tech
   Blog
2. **Aggregators:** Towards Data Science (Medium),
   KDnuggets
3. **Personal experts:** Andrej Karpathy, Andrew Gelman
   (statistics)
4. **Video:** YouTube channels like 3Blue1Brown and
   Google Cloud Tech

## 2. Collect: one place for everything
- Use a single hub so you're not visiting 10 sites.
- Tools: **RSS reader (Feedly)**, Notion, Pocket.
- Review **headlines only** at first.
- **Hook:** one information lake.

## 3. Process: 3 depths
**Hook: Glance, Skim, Dive.**

| Level | Question | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Is this topic relevant? | seconds |
| **Synopsis** | What's the core idea? (like an abstract) | 1-2 min |
| **Deep dive** | Read it all, follow links | only if vital |

**The funnel:** 10 articles -> 3 pass the title ->
1 gets a deep dive.

## Use generative AI as a copilot
- Cast a wider search net, summarize, or tutor you
  (ask follow-up questions).
- **Summary prompt idea:** give a URL + a persona (chief
  AI officer, staff engineer) + a format (3-sentence
  summary, strategic insight, questions for my team,
  notable quotes or data).
- **Weekly news prompt:** "Top 5 new articles this week
  on [topics], with title, source, summary, trend, link."
- **Competitive intel:** paste a competitor's release
  and ask what it means for your strategy.
- **Warning:** copilot, **not autopilot.** Check the
  output with your own judgment. Don't copy and paste.

## NotebookLM (Google)
- Gemini + retrieval (RAG) limited to **only the sources
  you upload.**
- Lecturer's flow: articles that reach the **synopsis**
  stage get uploaded to NotebookLM.
- Gives you: an overall summary, Q&A about the sources,
  an **audio overview** (podcast-style), study guides,
  and briefing docs.
- Turns 45-60 minutes of reading into ~20 minutes of
  listening.

## 30-second recall
Say SCP and the three depths. Give the 10 -> 3 -> 1
funnel. Name 5 sources you'd monitor.
