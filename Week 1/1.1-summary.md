# 1.1 What Is AI?
*AAI-501 · Week 1 · cross-checked against the transcript*

**One line:** AI is the study and construction of
**computational agents that act intelligently**, ideally
**rationally.**

## Why a definition is needed
Ask 5 people what AI is, get 5 answers:
1. Professors in a lab building robots and programs.
2. ChatGPT / the **generative AI boom** (hundreds of millions
   of users per day).
3. Advanced features in everyday systems (e.g., a **photo
   editor that auto-tags, organizes, edits** photos).
4. A robot that takes over the world.

All are **applications** of AI. The core definition:

## Definition
AI = the **study** and **construction** of **computational
agents that act intelligently.**
- **Study / synthesis** = understanding intelligence.
- **Construction** = building intelligent systems.
- So it's **understanding AND application.**

## Agent
In AI, an **agent is anything that can both perceive its
environment and act on it.** The idea is **not new.**

**Canonical flow:** Environment -> **perception (sensors)**
-> agent decides (what it wants to do) -> **actuators /
effectors** cause actions -> back to environment.
**Hook:** input -> decision -> output.

## 4 ways to define "intelligence"
Two dimensions: **thought vs action**, and **human vs
rationality.**

| | Human | Rational |
|---|---|---|
| **Thinking** | think like a human | think rationally |
| **Acting** | act like a human | **act rationally** (AI's focus) |

- **Thinking vs acting:** do we care about the **internal
  cognitive process** or about **effects**?
- **Rationality** = doing what **maximizes the expected
  outcome given current information.**
- Why not human? **Human behavior is often irrational.**
  Acting/thinking like a human means modeling **emotions and
  physical state** (tired? sleepy?).
- So the field is about **rationality**, specifically
  **acting rationally.**

**Catch:** rationality **does not guarantee the best outcome.**
- Picard (Star Trek): *"It is possible to commit no mistakes
  and still lose. That is not a weakness, that is life."*
- A rational agent **can't see the future.** Example: not
  investing in **Bitcoin in 2012** was rational given the
  available information, even though hindsight says it
  missed. You only use the information at hand.

## Physical vs digital (embodied vs software)
| Type | Body | Example (environment) |
|---|---|---|
| **Embodied AI** | **Physical body** | **Delivery robot:** perceives the physical world (a car ahead), navigates around it, delivers fastest |
| **Software agent / bot** | **Digital body only** | **Web crawler** in a Google data center: environment = web pages / the internet, uses algorithms to search and **index** pages |

- "We tend to think agents must be physical. Nothing could
  be further from the truth."
- Today's **"AI agents" / "agentic AI"** usually means
  **digital agents.**
- Digital vs physical is "one of the most important concepts
  to get."

## Applications (4 areas)
| Area | Examples (from lecture) |
|---|---|
| **Decision-making** (augment human understanding) | **Diagnosis:** tumor vs no tumor from **CT scans**. **Forecasting:** "buy more of product X so it's on the shelf." **Travel planning:** optimize getting goods A to B, turn messy human-set delivery routes into sensible ones |
| **Computer vision** | **Object detection** (puppy vs kitten vs duck) is the basic form. -> **Quality control:** factory making hundreds of thousands of **medicinal products**, check they're up to par and not defective. -> **Handwriting recognition:** **US Postal Service** reading handwritten/typed addresses (**St. Louis vs St. Paul**), "most impactful AI nobody thinks about" |
| **Robotics** | **Self-driving cars** (vision identifies pedestrians, vehicles, roads). **Manufacturing** robots (faster and more precise than humans, but AI is the **brain** telling them what to do). **Humanoids** (Tesla): do housework, shop; "holy grail," **not there yet** |
| **NLP** (natural language processing) | AI receives spoken/written human language and understands it. **ChatGPT** parses prompts in many languages. **Google Translate:** must understand **intent, sentiment, context** then translate (English -> Spanish). **Humanoids** need NLP to talk with us |

**Why NLP matters:** it makes AI **accessible to everyone.**
Not everyone will be a software engineer or data scientist,
but everyone can use language. Key to the **generative AI**
boom.
