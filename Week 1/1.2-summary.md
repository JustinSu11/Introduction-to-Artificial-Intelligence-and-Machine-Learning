# 1.2 A History of AI
*AAI-501 · Read time: 2 min*

**One line:** AI moves in **boom and bust cycles**
("AI winters"). Old ideas come back when data and
compute catch up.

## Foundations
- **Ancient:** Homer (700 BC) and ancient China imagined
  mechanical beings.
- **1600s Hobbes:** thought = **symbolic reasoning**
  (reasoning can be written as a repeatable process,
  like code).
- **Alan Turing** (godfather of AI):
  1. **Church-Turing thesis:** all computation can run
     on a Turing machine. Never disproven.
  2. **Turing test:** judges behavior (see below).
  3. Built an early computer, the **ACE**.
- **1943:** neural networks first proposed
  (McCulloch and Pitts). Nearly 100 years old.
- **1956 Dartmouth conference:** birthplace of AI, term
  "artificial intelligence" coined (Minsky, McCarthy,
  Shannon).

## Turing test
Player A = computer, B = human, C = interrogator.
If the computer convinces C it's human as often as the
human does, it passes.
**2025:** GPT-4.5 was judged human **73%** of the time
(UC San Diego study).

## Boom and bust (hook: Translate, Toy worlds, Rules)

| Boom | Era | Idea | Why it fell |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1950s | **Machine translation** (English-Russian, Cold War) | Failed on context. "Spirit is willing, flesh is weak" -> "whiskey is strong, meat is rotten." US cut funding 1966 |
| 2 | 1970s | **Microworlds:** shrink the world (Minsky's blocks, ELIZA chatbot) | Small worlds didn't generalize. Lighthill Report (UK, 1974) |
| 3 | 1980s | **Expert systems:** big if/then rule bases (MYCIN, Dendral) | Brittle, hard to maintain, retrain from scratch. Japan's Fifth Generation project failed. Winter in the 1990s |

*Lecturer's view: we're in the 4th boom.*

**Expert system parts:** knowledge base (rules) +
inference engine + working memory (facts).

## The comeback
- **1990s-early 2000s:** small data, classic statistics.
- **2005 Hadoop** -> big data era. **2006 AWS** -> cloud.
- **2012:** "data scientist" becomes a job.
- **2012 AlexNet** (Hinton + Krizhevsky) crushes
  ImageNet -> deep learning takes off. By 2014, vision
  is mostly neural networks.
- **2016 AlphaGo** beats the Go world champion.
- **2017 Transformers** (most fundamental advance in
  deep learning).
- **2021:** DALL-E, GitHub Copilot.
- **2022:** ChatGPT.
