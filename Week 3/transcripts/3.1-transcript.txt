Summary
Optimization with constraints: agents optimize decisions under real-world limits like resources and capacities.
Three optimization types: linear programming (continuous variables), mixed-integer programming (discrete variables), quadratic programming (non-linear relationships).
Factory problem example: models production of tables and chairs with resource and labor constraints.
Feasible region & revenue line: solutions lie within constraints; optimal revenue found at vertex points.
Simplex algorithm: efficient method to solve large-scale linear programs by exploring feasible vertices.
