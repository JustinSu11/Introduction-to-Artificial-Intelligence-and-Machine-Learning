Presentation 7.3: Model-free RL
AudioLinks to an external site. (16:00)
Summary
Explains model-free RL as learning from experience without full models.
Introduces Q-learning as an iterative method to approximate optimal Q-values.
Uses temporal-difference error for updating Q-values in a Q-table.
Describes Q-tables for simple problems and their scalability issues.
Mentions advanced methods: deep Q-networks, policy gradients, and actor-critic approaches.
